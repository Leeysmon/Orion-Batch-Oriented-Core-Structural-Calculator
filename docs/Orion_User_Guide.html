Orion

Batch Oriented Core Structural Calculator

User Guide · Version 3.0 · 2026
Developer: Dr. L. Hulijeli

1. What Orion does

Orion converts the alpha / beta core-orientation angles of many structures at once into true dip, dip direction and strike, using the drill-hole collar and survey data. You upload three tables (Collar, Survey, Structures) as CSV or XLSX, tell Orion which column holds which value, and press Calculate. Results appear in a sortable, filterable table, a 3D drill-trace viewer and a stereonet.

The app works offline; your data never leaves your computer.

3. Step 1 – Upload your data

Load the three tables, each as .csv or .xlsx (for Excel files you can pick the sheet).

  • Collar – one row per hole: hole ID, X, Y, Z (elevation) and maximum depth.
  • Survey – downhole surveys: hole ID, depth, azimuth, dip (and optionally X, Y, Z, hole size).
  • Structures – the structural measurements, one row per feature.

Example files are in the sample_data folder of the project.

4. Step 2 – Map the columns

When a table loads, a mapping card opens. For each required item, choose the column in your file that holds it. Orion tries to guess automatically; check each one.

Survey fields

Hole ID, X, Y, Z, Azimuth (hole trace), Dip (hole trace), Depth of survey, Hole size.

Structure fields

FieldMeaning
Hole ID, DepthWhere the structure is. A selector sets whether the depth is the top or the bottom of the structure.
Ori-linesolid = high certainty, dashed = moderate.
Typevein, breccia, contact, fracture, bedding, etc.
Core length / thickness (cm)Length along the core and true width of the structure.
Alpha, Beta, GammaOrientation angles measured on the core. Alpha and Beta are required; Gamma is optional.
Vein Min. 1–4 and %Vein minerals and their percentages.
Vein style, CommentFree text.
Tip: if rows are skipped, check that depth, alpha and beta are mapped to numeric columns (not text) and that hole IDs match exactly between tables.

Press Calculate once all three tables are mapped. Each section has a Help drop-down with short instructions.

5. Step 3 – Calculated structures table

  • Sort: click a column heading (click again to reverse).
  • Filter: type in the box beneath any heading. Combine categories with |, comma or semicolon, e.g. stringer | vn shows either. Exclude with !, e.g. !stringer. Numeric columns accept >10, <=45 and ranges 10..20.
  • Filters drive the viewers. As soon as a filter changes, the 3D viewer and the stereonet show only the filtered rows. Settings in the viewers can narrow the selection further, but never widen it.
  • Check boxes + Show in viewer: tick one or more rows and press Show in viewer to limit both viewers to those rows. Show all in viewer removes the limit; Uncheck all clears ticks.
  • Clear sort & filters resets the table.
  • Download CSV / XLSX saves the (filtered) results.

6. Step 4 – 3D viewer

Mouse controls

Buttons

Reset view, North-up plan (looks down with north at the top), Maximise, and Export (PNG, JPEG or PDF).

Display options

  • Hole – show all holes or one.
  • Colour planes by – e.g. Vein Min. 1 or Type.
  • Transparency and Plane size.
  • Depth interval – only structures within the chosen depth range are shown.
  • Show X/Y/Z axes – labelled axes with automatic tick spacing (1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 25, 50, 100 m). They meet at the lowest point of the trace so deep vs shallow is easy to judge.
  • Show legend – click an entry to hide or show that category in both viewers.

Hanging wall / foot wall

Each plane is two-tone: the lighter colour with a solid border is the hanging-wall side; the darker colour with a dotted border is the foot-wall side, depending on which side you are viewing.

7. Selected structure and compass

Select a structure in the table, 3D viewer or stereonet to see its true dip, dip direction, strike and strike/dip notation, plus a compass showing strike (blue), dip direction (orange) and the drill-hole direction (white). Result precision switches between whole and decimal degrees.

8. Stereonet

  • Shows exactly the structures visible in the 3D viewer (table filters, depth interval, legend, checked rows).
  • The dashed red line is the average plane of the structures shown; its dip direction/dip and strike are listed below the plot, along with the mean pole.
  • Settings let you toggle great circles, poles, mean plane, mean pole and grid, and set line thickness and pole size.
  • Click a line or pole to select it – the table scrolls to its row. Export as PNG, JPEG or PDF.

9. Conventions

  • Strike follows the right-hand rule (dip is to the right of strike direction).
  • Alpha is the angle between the core axis and the structure; beta is measured around the core from the bottom-of-hole reference line.
  • Hanging wall is the upper side of a structure.
